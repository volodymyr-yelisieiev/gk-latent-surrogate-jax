## Grading guideline

The IML BSc grading guideline weights the package as follows, and every main section must pass:

- scientific work: 50%;
- scientific documentation and code: 10%;
- manuscript: 40%;
- plagiarism check: mandatory pass condition.

The BSc manuscript requirements are 5,000--10,000 words excluding references, story summary,
and appendices; an abstract of about 250 words; at least 1,000 words for introduction and related
work; at least 500 words for limitations and conclusion; at least five figures or tables; and the
mandatory story-summary answers, each limited to three sentences. Assessment emphasizes suitable
methods and metrics, reproducibility, reliable experiments, interpretation of uncertainty and
limitations, runnable and documented code, complete setup/data instructions, source-backed claims,
clear figures and tables, and professional scientific writing. A limited or negative result is
acceptable when it is executed, analyzed, interpreted, and documented rigorously.
