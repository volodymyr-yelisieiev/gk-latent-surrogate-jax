\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{chap:conclusion}

This thesis developed and audited a compact latent time-series surrogate for preprocessed
five-dimensional gyrokinetic turbulence snapshots. The JAX/Flax implementation covers a
channel-first data contract, Cyclone/KvikIO loading, a 128-dimensional encoder, diagnostic heads,
latent caching, GRU and causal Transformer sequence models, recursive rollouts, trajectory-balanced
metrics, and artifact lineage. Synthetic fixtures and a CPU fallback make the engineering path
testable without private data or a GPU. The result is a complete research instrument rather than a
claim that a small model replaces a gyrokinetic solver.

The scientific comparison was deliberately rebuilt after an integrity audit found incompatible
trajectory-split seeds in an earlier exploratory result. Those numbers were withdrawn. The
accepted evidence binds representation learning, cache construction, sequence training, validation
selection, and outer evaluation to a manifest-/byte-verified universe of 51 trajectories. It uses
five outer group folds and five matched training seeds. Within each fold, sequence checkpoints are
chosen by validation latent RMSE and the learned family is selected by the mean validation flux
RMSE over seeds. Transformer is selected in folds 0, 1, and 3; GRU is selected in folds 2 and 4.
This two-level rule is explicit and never opens outer-test evidence for selection.

The answer to the central research question is negative for the declared setting. The selected
learned models have aggregate flux RMSE 14.6729, whereas direct persistence of the observed flux
has 10.0207. The learned-minus-observed difference is +4.6522, with a 10,000-replicate hierarchical
bootstrap interval [+2.5788, +6.6470]. Every outer-fold point difference is positive, and the
hierarchically weighted fraction of improved trajectory runs is 31.6\%. The correct conclusion is
therefore that the compact latent surrogate does not improve the strong observed-diagnostic
baseline at this horizon. This is a bounded negative result, not evidence that latent surrogates or
learned temporal models can never be useful.

The secondary evidence is consistent with that interpretation. Decoded latent persistence has
flux RMSE 14.8049, and its latent MSE is 0.4307 compared with 0.4497 for the learned model. The
learned-minus-persistence latent difference is +0.0189 with interval [-0.0699,+0.1332], so no
latent-dynamics advantage is established. The diagnostic-head oracle, supplied with true future
latents, reaches 12.3732: it exposes a material gap in the representation-to-diagnostic pipeline,
but it remains worse than observed persistence and does not isolate a single bottleneck. Spectral
relative errors and shape correlations likewise favour observed persistence, especially for
$\code{kyspec}$, while their separate reporting avoids a misleading pooled score.

The methodological contribution is as important as the numerical result. The stage ledger records
230 accepted and 25 skipped unselected-family slots; selection records state that test evidence was
not opened; each stage has resolved configuration and artifact hashes; and W\&B is explicitly
disabled rather than silently missing. The release manifest is path-sanitized and tied to the
frozen source tag, dataset revision, universe hash, fold hash, bootstrap seed, and result tables.
These controls make a less favourable conclusion more credible than a stronger number obtained from
an inconsistent split. They also provide a practical template for future model changes: freeze a
new protocol ID, bind a new universe, and repeat the same audit rather than editing the old result.

The claims remain intentionally narrow. The experiment does not establish full-field
reconstruction, long-horizon stability, physical-unit accuracy, solver speedup, generalization to
other regimes, or a direct numerical comparison with GyroSwin. Raw data, checkpoints, caches, and
trajectory rows require authorized server access. A future study should first improve the spatial
representation and diagnostic heads, then test a genuinely unseen trajectory set or a new nested
universe under a separately frozen protocol. Until a learned model reliably beats observed
persistence across such revisions, persistence remains the appropriate reference and a complex
architecture should not be presented as a surrogate success merely because its outputs are finite.
